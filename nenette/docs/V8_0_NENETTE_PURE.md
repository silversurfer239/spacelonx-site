# Nénette AI V8.0 — Nénette Pure

V8.0 replaces the fragmented terminal-first experience with a unified SpacelonX intelligence cockpit.

## Unified dashboard
- Executive overview
- Live market and on-chain pool price
- Wallet and portfolio snapshot
- Liquidity and exit realism
- Staking simulation
- Tokenomics
- Trust and security
- Roadmap
- Nénette intelligence
- Project center
- Reports

## Staking
Bronze, Silver, Gold and Diamond are simulation tiers. No smart contract is called. Stake, unstake and claim buttons remain disabled until a verified contract is integrated.

## Safety
All dashboard analysis is read-only. No seed phrase, private key or transaction signature is requested.
